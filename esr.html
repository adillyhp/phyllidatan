The Green Games

event

illustration

internship

During my Internship at LolliBox Pte Ltd, I was involved in the design of all design collaterals for The Green Games at ESR BizPark @ Chai Chee, a sustainability-themed event that encouraged shoppers to learn about environmental responsibility through interactive games and an eco craft workshop.


Client: ESR
Deliverables: Key Visuals, Event Poster, Game Backdrops, Game Passport

THE APPROACH

The designs were created to establish a cohesive visual identity while making sustainability education engaging, approachable, and enjoyable for participants of all ages.
