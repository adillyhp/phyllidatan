Our Blazing Summer

2d animation

personal project

Our Blazing Summer is an animation that follows Blaire as she reflects on a past friendship with Blaze, who was once her closest companion in high school before a painful fallout drove them apart.

STORYBOARD

THE APPROACH

Through a montage of scenes from their past and present that are both contrasting and parallels to each other, this film explores Blaire’s lingering regret over their lost connection and quiet longing for reconciliation.
