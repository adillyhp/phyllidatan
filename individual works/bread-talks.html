This brand identity video for Bread Talk aims to present the bakery as a trendy brand.


*This is a conceptual project and is not affiliated with Bread Talk in any official capacity.

STORYBOARD

THE APPROACH

By featuring a story of a girl dreaming about Bread Talk's bread, I focused on conveying that speciality in the bread's taste, feel and appearance.


While still incorporating features of Bread Talk's existing branding with the use their colour palette, and a character design inspired by their mascot, I aimed to create a design that is more visually appealing to a younger audience. Using organic and imperfect shapes along with textures, I created a visual aesthetic that feels imperfect but authentic.
