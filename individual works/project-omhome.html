Project OmHome

illustration

volunteer

Project OmHome is a self- initiated VIA project from Victoria Junior College, which was in collaboration with Children’s Aid Society Singapore, aimed at raising funds and awareness for children and youth in need. I worked with the team to design merchandise, with all proceeds going towards Children’s Aid Society.

THE APPROACH

I developed their mascot Omie, a confident, ambitious, and brave dinosaur who serves as a source of guidance and support for children in need. The visual direction features a muted pastel colour palette, creating a soft and approachable tone that enhances the character’s cuteness while remaining sensitive and appropriate to the cause.
