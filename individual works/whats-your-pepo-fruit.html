What's your Pepo fruit?

website

quiz

personal project

What’s Your Pepo Fruit? is a personality quiz website that reimagines friendship archetypes as quirky pepo fruits. Through a conversational chat- based experience, users answer a series of everyday social scenarios before receiving a fruit character that reflects their personality.

THE APPROACH

Using characters based on pepo fruits like melons, gourds, and other fruits with many seeds, the project combines humour, character design, and interaction to create a fun and shareable experience.
